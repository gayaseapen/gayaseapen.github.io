Multimedia Production

Old Media in New Worlds

"Old Media in New Worlds" is a podcast-series produced by students in DCD 303: Sonic Storytelling as a limited series that creatively juxtaposes the culture of old and new media forms using methods of sonic storytelling. Across distinct episodes of approximately 8-10 minutes, students observe how older media practices—ranging from CDs, photo albums, and arcade games—have either significantly shaped popular forms (like social media or online gaming) in the present, or have made a comeback as nostalgic or hobby-based practices. Inclusive of personal stories, research sources, and expert commentary, these episodes stand out for their unique use of sounds to embellish oral/aural modes of storytelling and expression, fit for the digital era.
