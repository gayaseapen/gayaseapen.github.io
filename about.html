Gayas Eapen

Assistant Professor, Digital Culture and Design

Coastal Carolina University



Bio: Dr. Gayas Eapen is an assistant professor of Digital Culture and Design at Coastal Carolina University in South Carolina, USA. His scholarly work concerns media in public, sonic technologies of the street, and digital platforms. He is currently working on a book project that attempts to grasp media technologies that often intersect with ethno-religious, caste, and gendered conflicts and tensions. In the classroom, he is fascinated by discussions on technologies and infrastructures that shape us into who we are. His work has been published in the scholarly journal Asiascape: Digital Asia and in the De Gruyter Handbook of Digital Culture. Prior to Coastal, he has taught as a graduate instructor at NC State University where he completed his PhD. In a previous life, Dr. Eapen worked as a journalist in India and lectured in the University of Delhi and University of Calicut.
