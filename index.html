Gayas Eapen

Digital Media Researcher and Practitioner

Hi! I'm a scholar and practitioner of digital and sonic media. I have been researching and writing about media practices such as sound trucks on city-streets, performances by mobile artists, and related cultures that have transformed my understanding of media. This work has been carried out in Northern India.

I am also a media ethnographer and storyteller and have worked on telling stories through interactive, visual, and sonic media.My research into media practices and storytelling has intersected around a project around motorycle culture in the American South.

More recently, I have been thinking and writing about sound cultures on social media platforms that hare being shaped by generative AI tools and algorithms. My research has taken different forms from journal articles to critically informed web-based multimedia projects.
