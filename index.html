Gayas Eapen

Digital Media Researcher and Practitioner

Hi! I'm a scholar and practitioner of digital and sonic media. I have been researching and writing about sound trucks on city-streets, performances by mobile artists, and related cultures that have transformed my understanding of "media." I have largely carried out this research in different parts of Northern India.

I am also a media ethnographer and storyteller and have worked on telling stories through interactive, visual, and sonic media. My research on media practices got me interested in working on a project around motorycle cultures in the American South.

Recently, I have been thinking and writing about sound cultures on social media platforms that are being shaped by generative AI tools and recommender algorithms. My research has taken different forms from journal articles to web-based multimedia projects.
